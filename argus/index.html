01 // Who We Are

More than a robot.
A system of thinking.

"ARGUS brings together people who want to understand how things work, and then build something better. We approach robotics as a complete engineering discipline, combining mechanical systems, electronics, programming, strategy, testing, and collaboration. Our objective is not simply to produce a working machine. It is to understand every decision behind it, learn from every iteration, and continuously improve our approach."

Representing Dr. B.R. Ambedkar School of Specialized Excellence (CM SHRI School, Sector 11, Rohini, Delhi), Team 012 entered the National Robotics League 2026 to push beyond textbook engineering. From rapid prototyping and raw acrylic chassis fabrication to custom ESP-NOW telemetry firmware, every component is rigorously documented, tested, and iterated upon evidence.

01 // SUBSYSTEM PROPULSION
Drivetrain

Differential tank drive powered by two high-torque DC motors. Features ramp-limited TankDrive firmware control, dual slow/fast driver modes, gyro heading-hold assist, laser-cut clear acrylic chassis plates, and four treaded high-traction wheels.

02 // SUBSYSTEM MANIPULATION
Two-Servo Arm

Two-servo arm integrated with a programmed preset ladder: Floor, Carry, Star, High, and Apex. Features one-press rung stepping, RT-gated manual rate control, and an engineered arm lift travel from a 115-degree top stop to a 180-degree floor pickup.

03 // SUBSYSTEM END EFFECTOR
Claw Mechanism

Precision gripper calibrated from 0-degree wide open to 105-degree max-grip close, measured directly on the robot. Iteratively tuned across five distinct testing cycles from 90° to 105° for maximum CORE retention during sudden acceleration.

04 // SUBSYSTEM AVIONICS & CONTROL
Electronics & Telemetry

HEXA Command Hub (ESP32-S3) paired directly to our custom handheld controller over raw ESP-NOW on channel 11 — zero WiFi router required. Features an MCP3008 ADC for live battery/current telemetry, LSM6DSOX 6-DoF gyro, status OLED, and NeoPixel LED indicators.

05 // SUBSYSTEM MISSION ELEMENT
Payload Bay

90 mm, approximately 150 g FDM-printed Team 012 custom shell. Secured inside a dedicated cargo bay and delivered to the field Drop Zone via a separate autonomous release mechanism.

06 // INTEGRATED SYSTEM THE MACHINE
The Whole Machine

Built for fast CORE cycling, reliable Payload delivery, and a decisive LEVEL UP endgame climb. Strictly compliant with official NRL specifications: stays within the 18-inch starting volume and 8 kg weight ceiling.

Claw Grip Iteration Story

Initial testing with standard 90-degree servo travel dropped CORE elements under lateral robot acceleration. Over five successive prototyping and telemetry-tested cycles, the claw geometry and silicone-lined grip pads were remodeled.

Final calibrated travel: 0-degree open to 105-degree max-grip close. This 15-degree mechanical overdrive ensures zero slip even during 90-degree gyro-assisted snap turns.

ITERATION 1: 90° (Baseline) ➔ ITERATION 3: 98° ➔ ITERATION 5: 105° (Measured Max Grip)
Programming & OpModes

All autonomous and TeleOp opmodes are executed strictly within the official NRL project environment, maintaining the engine completely untouched in full adherence with Rule R8.2.

Control features: Joystick stick driving with deadband filtration and exponential response curves, RT-gated manual arm rate stepping, LSM6DSOX gyro heading-hold, instant stop braking, and an automatic motor cutoff safety if the ESP-NOW radio link is lost.

01 // Who We Are

More than a robot.
A system of thinking.

"ARGUS brings together people who want to understand how things work, and then build something better. We approach robotics as a complete engineering discipline, combining mechanical systems, electronics, programming, strategy, testing, and collaboration. Our objective is not simply to produce a working machine. It is to understand every decision behind it, learn from every iteration, and continuously improve our approach."

Representing Dr. B.R. Ambedkar School of Specialized Excellence (CM SHRI School, Sector 11, Rohini, Delhi), Team 012 turns classroom physics and software engineering into competition-ready hardware.

01 // SUBSYSTEM PROPULSION
Drivetrain

Differential tank drive powered by two DC motors, ramp-limited TankDrive control, slow/fast driver modes, gyro heading-hold assist, clear acrylic chassis plates, and four treaded wheels.

02 // SUBSYSTEM MANIPULATION
Two-Servo Arm

Two-servo arm with preset ladder (floor, carry, star, high, apex), one-press rung stepping, and arm lift travel from 115-degree top stop to 180-degree floor.

03 // SUBSYSTEM END EFFECTOR
Claw Mechanism

Gripper 0-degree open to 105-degree max-grip close, measured on the robot; tuned across five iterations from 90 to 105 degrees.

04 // SUBSYSTEM AVIONICS
Electronics & Telemetry

HEXA Command Hub (ESP32-S3) paired to its handheld controller over ESP-NOW, channel 11, no WiFi router needed. MCP3008 ADC live battery/current telemetry, LSM6DSOX gyro, status OLED, NeoPixel LED.

05 // SUBSYSTEM MISSION ELEMENT
Payload Bay

90 mm, about 150 g FDM-printed Team 012 shell, delivered to the field Drop Zone by a separate claw mechanism.

06 // INTEGRATED SYSTEM THE MACHINE
The Whole Machine

Built for fast CORE scoring, reliable Payload delivery, and a strong LEVEL UP endgame. Stays within the 18-inch starting volume and 8 kg limit.

Claw Iteration Story

Claw close was tuned across five documented iterations from 90° to 105° measured max grip on the robot, eliminating CORE slippage during rapid 90° snap turns.

90° BASELINE ➔ 5 ITERATIONS ➔ 105° MAX GRIP
Programming & Rule R8.2

OpModes only inside the official NRL environment (engine untouched per Rule R8.2): stick driving with deadband & response curves, RT-gated arm rate control, gyro heading-hold, 90° snap turns, instant stop, and automatic drive cutoff on radio-link loss.

01 // Who We Are

More than a robot.
A system of thinking.

"ARGUS brings together people who want to understand how things work, and then build something better. We approach robotics as a complete engineering discipline, combining mechanical systems, electronics, programming, strategy, testing, and collaboration. Our objective is not simply to produce a working machine. It is to understand every decision behind it, learn from every iteration, and continuously improve our approach."

Design
